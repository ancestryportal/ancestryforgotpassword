Understanding Ancestry Forgot Password
======================================

The Ancestry forgot password scenario occurs when you cannot remember your Ancestry account password, preventing you from signing in to your account and accessing your family history research. Understanding what happens during the Ancestry forgot password recovery process helps you navigate it effectively and appreciate the security measures in place.

When you initiate an Ancestry forgot password recovery, Ancestry's systems guide you through a series of identity verification steps designed to confirm that you are the legitimate account owner. These verification steps are crucial for protecting your account from unauthorized access. The Ancestry forgot password recovery process requires access to the email address associated with your Ancestry account . If you can no longer access that email address, you will need to contact Ancestry support for help .

The Ancestry forgot password process is designed to be secure and user-friendly. Ancestry sends a six-digit verification code to your registered email address, which you must enter on the password reset page to verify your identity . This code must be entered within five minutes of receiving it, and it is case-sensitive . After successful verification, you can create a new password and regain access to your account.

The Ancestry forgot password situation is different from simply changing your password. If you are already signed in to your account, you can change your password through your account settings without going through the reset process . The reset process is specifically for users who cannot sign in because they have forgotten their password.

Prerequisites for Ancestry Forgot Password Recovery
===================================================

Before beginning the Ancestry forgot password recovery process, there are several prerequisites you need to be aware of to ensure a smooth experience. Proper preparation increases the likelihood of successful recovery and reduces frustration.

The primary prerequisite for Ancestry forgot password recovery is having access to the email address associated with your Ancestry account . Ancestry sends the password reset code to this email address, and you will need to access it to complete the verification process. If you can no longer access this email address, you will need to contact Ancestry support for help .

If you created your Ancestry account using Sign up with Apple and selected "Hide my Email," your account may be associated with a privaterelay.appleid.com email address created by Apple . In this case, you will need to find your Apple Relay Email to reset your password. You can find this email address by checking your Account settings if you can still access your account, or by going to Settings on your iPhone or iPad, selecting your name, choosing Sign in with Apple, and selecting Ancestry .

If you created your Ancestry account using Sign up with Google or Apple, you might not yet have an Ancestry password . In this case, you may need to create an Ancestry password before you can reset it. You can create a password through your Account settings if you can access your account, or through the password reset process if you cannot access your account .

Ensuring you have a stable internet connection is important for Ancestry forgot password recovery. The recovery process requires communication with Ancestry's servers, and a reliable connection prevents interruptions. If you are using a public or shared device, ensure you are in a private environment before beginning the Ancestry forgot password recovery process.

Web-Based Ancestry Forgot Password Recovery
===========================================

The web-based Ancestry forgot password recovery process is the most common method for resetting your password from a computer. This approach works on any modern web browser and requires no additional software installation.

To begin the web-based Ancestry forgot password recovery, navigate to the Ancestry sign-in page. You can find the sign-in button in the top-right corner of most Ancestry pages . If you do not see the sign-in button, you or someone else may already be signed in. To switch accounts, click the profile in the top-right corner and choose Sign Out .

On the sign-in page, click the "Don't know your password?" link . This initiates the Ancestry forgot password recovery process. You will be prompted to enter the email address associated with your Ancestry account. Enter your email address and click Continue .

Do not close or navigate away from this page. If Ancestry has an account linked to that email address, they will send a password reset code within five minutes, and you will enter it on this page . Only request the code once within five minutes. Requesting it multiple times may cause delays or confusion.

In a separate tab or window, check your email inbox for a six-digit code from Ancestry . If you do not receive the email, check your spam or junk folder. The email may also be delayed, so wait a few minutes before requesting a new code. If you still do not receive the code, your account may be linked to another email address. Try your other email addresses .

If you still cannot receive the code, contact Ancestry support for assistance . If you are using Sign in with Apple, you may need to find your Apple Relay Email as described in the prerequisites section .

Enter the six-digit code in the password reset page and click Continue . The code is case-sensitive and must be used within two hours. If it has been longer than two hours, request a new code .

On the Reset Password page, enter your new password twice to confirm it, then click Continue . Ancestry passwords are case-sensitive and must meet specific requirements. They must be at least eight characters long, contain at least one number, and contain at least one letter or special symbol . Avoid using common or well-known passwords.

After you click Continue, your password will be updated, and Ancestry will sign you in to your account .

Mobile App Ancestry Forgot Password Recovery
============================================

The Ancestry mobile app provides a convenient way to reset your password from your smartphone or tablet. The app is available for both Android and iOS devices and offers the same password recovery functionality as the web version.

To begin the Ancestry forgot password recovery through the mobile app, open the Ancestry app and tap the "Sign In" button . On the app's sign-in page, tap "Forgot Password?" .

You will be prompted to enter the email address associated with your Ancestry account. Enter your email address and tap Continue . Do not close or navigate away from this screen. If Ancestry has an account linked to that email address, they will send a password reset code within five minutes, and you will enter it here .

Check your email inbox for a six-digit code from Ancestry . If you do not receive the code, you can tap "Resend code" . If you still do not receive the code, your account may be linked to another email address. Try your other email addresses .

If you are using Sign in with Apple and selected "Hide my Email," you may need to find your Apple Relay Email to receive the reset code . You can find this email address by going to Settings on your iPhone or iPad, selecting your name, choosing Sign in with Apple, and selecting Ancestry .

Enter the code and tap Continue . Enter your new password twice to confirm it, then tap Submit . Your password will be updated, and Ancestry will sign you in to the app .

Creating an Ancestry Password for Google or Apple Accounts
==========================================================

If you created your Ancestry account using Sign up with Google or Apple, you might not yet have an Ancestry password . In some cases, you may need to create an Ancestry password, such as when you need to delete your Ancestry account or when you want to sign in using your email and password instead of Google or Apple .

If you can access your Ancestry account, you can create a password through your Account settings . Go to Account settings and, under Account information, select "Create password." You will be prompted to enter your email address, and Ancestry will send a password reset code to that email address. Enter the code and create a new password .

If you cannot access your Ancestry account, you can create a password through the password reset process . Go to the Ancestry sign-in page, select "Don't know your password?" and enter your email address. Ancestry will send a code to your email address with further instructions. From the email message, select "Create a new password here" and follow the prompts .

Creating an Ancestry password will not change your Google or Apple password . The Ancestry password is separate and only used for signing in to Ancestry with your email address and password.

Troubleshooting Ancestry Forgot Password Issues
===============================================

Even with careful execution, users may encounter issues during the Ancestry forgot password recovery process. Understanding common problems and their solutions helps resolve issues quickly.

If you are not receiving the verification code during Ancestry forgot password recovery, check your spam or junk folder for the email . The email may also be delayed, so wait a few minutes before requesting a new code. If you still do not receive the code, your account may be linked to another email address. Try your other email addresses .

If you receive multiple password reset codes, use the most recent code; only the newest one works . Each new code invalidates the previous ones.

If you have recently reset your password and your new password is not working, ensure you are entering the new password and not using a saved or cached password . Clear your browser's cache and cookies and try again . If you are using Sign in with Google or Apple, sign in with your Google or Apple password instead of your Ancestry password .

If you do not know your username, try signing in with your email address . Your username is often the same as your email address or a variation of it. You can also find your username in your account settings if you can still access your account.

If you can no longer access the email address associated with your Ancestry account, you will need to contact Ancestry support for help . Support can assist with resetting your password and updating your account information.

If a page or button is not working during the password reset process, clear your browser's cache and cookies and try again . If that does not work, try using a different browser or device .
